# Delivery Delay and Escalation Policy

## Purpose
This policy governs the operational handling of delivery delays and lost shipments.

## Requirements
- If a shipment is delayed more than 48 hours beyond the expected delivery date, the case must be escalated to the operations team.
- The customer must receive a status update with the latest carrier information.
- Refunds over $100 require human approval.
- Lost packages require a carrier confirmation and evidence of the last successful scan.

## Evidence
- Tracking status and last scan details are required before a final recommendation is made.
- Prior delivery records may be used to confirm if the delay is recurring.
